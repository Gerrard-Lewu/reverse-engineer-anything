L2: # Connect HubSpot to Explee
L3: 
L4: Explee sends every hot lead to HubSpot the moment it appears. Each hot lead becomes a contact and a deal in the pipeline you choose, with the full outreach conversation logged on the timeline. Your team works leads in HubSpot as usual — no copy-paste, no CSV imports.
L5: 
L6: ## Before you start
L7: 
L8:   * A HubSpot account where you can install apps (super admin, or a user with App Marketplace access).
L9:   * An Explee project with outreach running.

L40: ## Data Explee accesses
L41: 
L42: The integration uses HubSpot OAuth with the minimum set of permissions:
L43: 
L44: HubSpot data  | Access  | Used for
L45: --- | --- | ---
L46: Contacts  | Read and write  | Create the hot lead as a contact; match by email to avoid duplicates
L47: Companies  | Read and write  | Attach the lead's company; match by domain to avoid duplicates
L48: Deals  | Read and write  | Create the deal and list your pipelines for the stage picker
L49: ## FAQ
L50: 
L51: Which leads does Explee send to HubSpot?
L52: 
L53: Hot leads only — people who replied to your outreach with interest. Explee does not copy your whole lead list into HubSpot.
L54: 
L55: What does Explee create in HubSpot?
L56: 
L57: A contact (name, title, company, LinkedIn), a company, and a deal in the pipeline you chose. The deal timeline gets a note with the reason the lead is hot and the full email conversation.
L58: 
L59: Will I get duplicates?
L60: No. Contacts are matched by email and companies by domain. A lead that replies twice still produces one contact and one deal.
L61: 
L62: Does Explee read my CRM data?
L63: 
L64: Explee only searches contacts and companies to prevent duplicates, and reads your deal pipelines so you can pick a stage. It never reads deal contents, emails, or other records.
L65: 
L66: How do I disconnect?
L67: Go to Settings → Integrations and click Disconnect. Explee revokes its own access on the HubSpot side. You can also uninstall the app from HubSpot under Settings → Integrations → Connected apps.
L68: 
L69: What does it cost?
L70: 
L71: The HubSpot integration is included at no extra cost on every Explee plan except Starter.
